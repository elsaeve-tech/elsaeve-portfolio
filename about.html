About Elsa

Hi, I'm Elsa. I'm an artist living on the island of Kauaʻi, where much of my work begins with a walk, a view, or a small detail that catches my attention. Painting has become my way of slowing down and preserving those moments, while illustration lets me explore storytelling, imagination, and collaboration.

When I'm not creating, you'll usually find me exploring the island, practicing yoga, petting your dog, or collecting inspiration for whatever comes next.
